Why Check Vehicle Hypothecation & Loan Status?

When purchasing a pre-owned car or bike, it is crucial to verify whether the vehicle is free from bank loans or legal dues. Hypothecation means the vehicle is pledged to a bank or financial institution as security for a loan. Driving or buying a vehicle with an active unpaid loan without proper clearance can lead to legal complications.

  • Ensure Ownership Clarity: Confirm if the previous owner has fully paid off the vehicle loan.
  • No Dues Verification: Check if there are any pending overdue amounts or active monthly installments.
  • NOC Availability: Easily verify and generate reference documents confirming cleared financial status.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is vehicle hypothecation?
Hypothecation indicates that the vehicle was bought using a car/bike loan and is legally registered under a bank's security until the loan is fully repaid.

Q2: How can I remove hypothecation from my RC?
Once you clear your loan, the bank issues a No Objection Certificate (NOC). You can submit this NOC along with form 35 to your local Regional Transport Office (RTO) to get the bank's name removed from your Registration Certificate.
